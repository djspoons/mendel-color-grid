# CC0 1.0 dedication for the team-shot photo set

Commit this file next to the photos (for example as `photos/CC0-DEDICATION.md`).
Fill in the two bracketed values when the set is committed; nothing else changes.

have waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights to the photographs
in this folder, identified by the shot ids S01 to S20 and committed in the
repository at [REPOSITORY URL] on [COMMIT DATE, YYYY-MM-DD].

The waiver is the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.
Its full legal text is at https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
and applies to every photograph in this folder.

What the dedication does not cover:

- Personality, privacy or publicity rights of the people shown. These are
  handled by the signed or recorded releases listed in `manifest.json`, each of
  which agrees to CC0 publication.
- Trademarks or third-party artwork that appear in a photo. Reshoot or remove
  those photos instead of relying on the dedication.

Each photographer confirms it by ticking `cc0_dedicated` for their photos in
`manifest.json`, which `validate_photos.py` requires.
